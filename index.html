      GRONKILLION is an independent football trivia game and is not affiliated with or endorsed by the NFL.

      How to play

      Seven plays make one drive. Each play is an NFL prompt with lots of correct answers. Type one in, and dig deep: the less obvious your answer, the more yards you gain.

      The play clock

      • You get a 3-second snap count to read the prompt, then 25 seconds to answer.
      • A wrong or unrecognized answer doesn't end the play. Keep trying until the clock hits zero.
      • Your first accepted answer locks in the play. If we fix a typo for you, submit once more to confirm it.

      Rarity scoring

      Rarity tiers are curated estimates of how likely an average fan is to think of each answer. They are not live player statistics.

      The field

      Every point moves the ball downfield. A perfect 700-point drive reaches the end zone for a touchdown.

      Typing tips

      • Capitalization, punctuation and suffixes like Jr. or III don't matter.
      • Last names work when only one well-known player has that name. Otherwise, add a first name.
      • Well-known nicknames work, and so do team nicknames, cities and abbreviations like KC or SF.

      Modes

      • Daily Challenge: the same seven plays for everyone each UTC day. One scored attempt per day.
      • Unlimited Mode: play as many drives as you like.
